Masar Mall Project

Electrical BIM Engineer at Zamil Projects, responsible for developing, maintaining, and coordinating the BIM model for Masar Mall, KSA.

Responsibilities

  • Developing and managing Revit project environments, including families, templates, view templates, schedules, sheets, and project standards.
  • Performing multidisciplinary BIM coordination across Architectural, Structural, Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing disciplines to resolve design conflicts.
  • Conducting clash detection, issue tracking, and coordination reviews to keep models accurate and construction-ready.
  • Creating Dynamo scripts to automate repetitive tasks and streamline project workflows.
  • Performing BIM quality control and model audits to ensure compliance with company and project standards.
